## Any harness. One continuous workflow.

Your work shouldn’t be tied to one AI assistant—or the app you use to run it.

**Taskrunner is designed to connect any agent harness:** the software an AI uses to read files, run tools, and work on your behalf.

That means a conversation can start in one place and continue somewhere else.

> “Look at my last session and pick up where we left off.”

With supported conversation history available through Taskrunner, the next assistant can find where you stopped, recover earlier decisions, and look deeper when it needs more context. You keep moving without writing a handover.

**And that’s just the beginning:**

- **Switch assistants without rebuilding the backstory.**
- **Bring in another model for a second opinion.**
- **Give two models the same task and compare their work.**
- **Check the history yourself:** ask what an earlier assistant said and how the next one found it.

Claude and Codex are one example. The bigger idea is freedom to choose your tools while keeping your work connected.

**Different models. Different harnesses. Your project carries on.**
